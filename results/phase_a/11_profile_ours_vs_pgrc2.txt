Findings:
  1. libgomp spin is NOT our problem -- PgRC2 spins MORE (24.21 vs 22.50) and
     still finishes in a third the time. An earlier conclusion blaming barrier
     spin after A1 is withdrawn.
  2. The differentiator is page faults: 6.4x, accounting for essentially all of
     our system time. Not thread creation (99 vs 23 is far too few to matter)
     and not preemption (context switches are identical).
  3. Source: preset 9 asks for a 64 MB LZMA dictionary regardless of stream
     size; seven probes over six streams >= 2 MB is ~2.7 GB of allocate-touch-
     free per file. PgRC2 sizes dictionaries per stream (1/8/16/64 MB).
  4. Our compression is 6.7x more of the profile than theirs, because they do
     not probe their large streams at all.
  5. We are FASTER at the algorithm (assembly 1.64 s vs their 2.75 s) and
     slower at everything around it.
